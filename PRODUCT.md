# SmartTube Sub+

## Product
- **Name:** SmartTube Sub+ (`com.heyhacene.smarttube.subplus`)
- **Platform:** Android TV (`stbeta` flavor)
- **What it does:** Fork of SmartTube for Android TV adding contextual AI subtitle translation (Gemini / OpenAI / Ollama), search language filtering, player audio-track/dubbing access, and untranslated YouTube video titles.

## Brand & Icon Brief
- **Visual lineage:** Clearly evokes the original SmartTube icon (play-button / TV screen DNA) while remaining immediately distinguishable at 3m distance on an Android TV launcher.
- **Color strategy:** Deep dark background with a crisp, high-contrast vivid accent for maximum legibility on TV.
- **Hard constraints:**
  - Zero blurred fake TV background or mockup frame — clean edge-to-edge artwork filling the entire 1:1 icon (`app_icon.png`) and 16:9 TV banner (`app_banner.png`).
  - No miniature illegible text.
  - Instantly recognizable as a distinct SmartTube Sub+ build rather than stock SmartTube.
